# Leicester quarter block plan

The snag-marked gap behind the northeast Lord Street frontage now has two
separate provisional assets: `southport.leicester-quarter.frontage-placeholders`
and `southport.leicester-quarter.public-realm`. They continue Stanley and Bath
Streets through Seabank Road, Gordon Street and Johnson Street toward Leicester
Street. The northern limit reaches Booth Street and St Marie’s grounds so whole
building envelopes fit inside the bounded area.

## Evidence and measurements

Actual Google Maps GPU 3D imagery was displayed and inspected from three rotated
headings on 25 October 2026. Registered north-up satellite captures and July 2026
Bath/Seabank and Gordon Street panoramas support the plan. Private captures and
registration overlays are outside Git in `../references/mapping-sol-trial` and
`../output/mapping-sol-trial`. Source URLs, capture dates, pixel/coordinate inputs,
height calculations and missing coverage are retained in each asset’s research
records.

The retained OSM extract supplies the street network and several outlines, but
omits many rear ranges. Simple aerial-derived envelopes fill those gaps. These
are broad visible volumes, not cadastral property divisions. Flat tops describe
estimated height bounds rather than reconstructed roofs. Plan positions remain
uncertain by approximately 1–3 m; heights by approximately 3 m. Low-resolution
photogrammetry and roof parallax cannot establish precise wall positions.

Three mapped roof edges crossed the observed Stanley/Gordon carriageways.
Their provisional wall bounds are trimmed within the stated uncertainty to keep
footways and roads clear. Original seed outlines remain retained for comparison.
Two overlapping attached church ranges were removed during construction rather
than appending volumes already represented by its T-shaped envelope.

## Ownership and viewing levels

Ground uses the existing Lord Street seam at Y=65 m and the Stanley ownership
cut at X=250 m, with the latter’s northern end at Y=175 m explicitly excluded.
New ground extends to X=218–610 m and Y=65–230 m. All existing and new building
footprints cut out the walkable floor. Private courts remain restrained plain
surfaces; garden reservations are estimated rather than detailed landscape work.

Road unions are offset continuously before clipping to four streaming parts.
Kerbs and road paint share those curves. Bath and Stanley paired markings
continue the adjoining source geometry. The unmarked Lord Street handover stays
clear; other current restrictions and furniture are deferred. Interior levels
extend the existing Stanley datum and remain unsurveyed.

Detail, Walk and District deliberately retain the same simple building prisms
and ground outlines, with reduced ground map sizes. The declared independent
budgets are 10,000 building triangles and 24,000 ground triangles per level.
Adding future architecture must replace these placeholders on the agreed plan.

Validators check closed positive building volumes, all new/new and new/existing
footprint intersections, road clearance, footprint floor exclusions, shared
ownership cuts, road-centre continuity, finite nondegenerate exported coordinates
and same-facing coplanar overlap. These checks establish geometry ownership and
access, not survey or architectural accuracy.

## Review record, 25 October 2026

The final snag-box check includes Gordon House and its courts across Leicester
Street, extending the eastern ownership cut to X=610 m in a fourth ground part.
The OSM parking aisle south of Gordon House ran on its wall; the retained aerial
shows the aisle between the frontage and street. Its provisional centreline
moves approximately 3 m south, with the original seed kept alongside it.

The assembled published overview was compared with the three actual 3D orbit
captures and registered aerial overlays. The street grid, open courts and
relative low/tall range groups agree at block scale. Detailed roof forms, exact
lodging-house divisions, fine sheds and courtyard design remain deferred.
Actual GPU viewer checks verified all new building tiles and every visible new
ground part at forced Detail, Walk and District. The building prisms collapse to
one identical representation; ground textures change while boundaries stay fixed.
Pedestrian views across the Stanley ownership seam show continuous kerbs,
markings and paving.

The exported uniform-level coplanar audit includes the neighbouring Lord and
Stanley block assets. Its initial pass found no conflicts involving this new
quarter; two tiny findings wholly inside the pre-existing Lord corridor are
outside this scope and remain recorded in the private audit. Exported boundary
probes check both sides of adjoining road and footway cuts at all three levels.
The private comparison images and complete audit outputs are in
`../output/mapping-sol-trial`.
