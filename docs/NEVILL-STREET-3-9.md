# 3–9 Nevill Street — Silcock’s Jackpot range

The red-brick range between Occulo and Empire comprises six tall first-floor windows, nine narrower second-floor windows, and a separate projecting terminal bay. Its street facade has segmental stone arches, slender sash joinery, pilaster capitals, a continuous dentilled cornice, a small terminal pediment and recessed burgundy shopfronts. Geometry represents the exterior; interiors and unreadable advertising are omitted.

Street View from three positions (July 2026), a Savills frontal photograph and Maps place photos support the facade. Google photogrammetry was orbited from four directions and inspected in plan for the truncated hip, raised roof cover and two rear extensions. The taller wing's pale windowed side faces the courtyard; its outer end is gabled. Mapillary supplied an opposite-facing street image but no useful courtyard view. The auction PDF was inaccessible and no unseen plan is treated as evidence. Links, observation dates, measured image ratios and uncertainties are in the asset's research records.

The adopted frontage is 16.8 m, the main depth 9 m and the cornice 11.6 m above the local base. These are image-derived estimates, not a survey. Existing Occulo registration is retained: panorama angle estimates suggest a possible 2–3 m discrepancy, but uncertain panorama calibration is insufficient reason to move neighbours independently. Rear window dimensions, finishes, carving depth and roof-cover construction remain provisional. No invented chimney pots, rear staircases or hidden doors are added.

The existing west block placeholder is split around this range and its open rear court. Empire and the small Occulo rear infill retain separate placeholder ownership; other Lord Street volumes remain untouched. Streets and private yard paving belong to public-realm work, not this building asset.

Detail and Walk preserve apertures and modest architectural geometry. District is an independently authored 84-triangle envelope retaining the hip, terminal bay, wings and court. It bakes frontage to 512² and rear/roof to 256² atlases, with fixed per-part topology budgets and a total 2 MB download / 7 MB GPU budget. The current Walk mesh intentionally matches Detail; a future reduction can omit fine capitals, guards and lettering without changing District complexity.

Validation checks float32 triangles, closed outward roof slabs, 17 unobscured upper windows, packed textures and scoped coplanar shell overlap. Exported Detail, Walk and District are reviewed alongside front/rear/roof renders and assembled street views. Private reference images and review captures stay outside Git.

The level building floor uses the rough street height at the frontage anchor recorded in `research/layout.json`, with a 12 cm paving allowance and 2 cm threshold allowance. These are editable town-model controls, not a surveyed floor datum.
