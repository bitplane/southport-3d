# Former BHS, 17–23 Chapel Street

Provisional reconstructed exterior of the connected Chapel Street corner, Cambridge Arcade wing and Corporation Street gabled range. It represents retained fabric visible in published photographs, with temporary conversion works omitted; the proposed completed conversion is not modelled as fact. The Qlocal photographs published in 2025 show the arched and columned Corporation return and the half-timbered service gables. The February 2026 Southport Lead photograph shows scaffolding and a partly stripped canopy soffit; temporary scaffolding is omitted. Images and screenshots remain private. Source links and dated coverage are in `research/coverage.json` and `research/references.json`.

The existing OSM-derived block corners provide registration rather than a survey. The local rectangular construction coordinates span 28.4 × 46.8 m and are mapped bilinearly to the skewed four-corner plan. The gross rectangle is approximately 1,329 m²; Sefton's 2025 assessment gives a 1,340 m² site, a plausibility check only, not a measurement of the building. The wing, frontage and five transverse roofs were compared with four Google 3D bearings and closer imagery. Approximate wing width 14 m, gabled range 8.5 m and their intervening 5.9 m court are hypotheses inferred from obliques, not surveyed measurements. The Town Hall closes the rear boundary; the court is not asserted to be a through passage. Ridge/eave estimates have at least 1–2 m uncertainty. Its concealed junctions, detailed roof materials, individual window measurements, latest post-conversion condition and all hidden interior construction remain unresolved.

The revised master has projecting curved masonry and glazed bays, deep upper galleries, large arched openings, individual roof cutouts and framed dormers, five gabled service bays, and a modelled Chapel canopy. Historic features from the 2011 street panorama include the Corporation service door, shutter and timber gables; the unobscured Chapel photographs guide the retained stone frontage. Their capture dates are not confirmed by article publication dates. Contemporary conversion completion is not assumed. The exact carving, signage typography, entrance positions, concealed roof junctions and gallery depths are estimates; the revision schedule records photographic landmarks and uncertainty.

Walk retains the major bays, gallery columns, dormers, gable coves and opening divisions; small leadwork and louvre blades are omitted. District has independently authored curved bays, gallery and arch voids, roof cutouts, court and major silhouette. The current bounded limits are 140,000 Detail, 110,000 Walk and 1,200 District triangles, with at most 3 MB District download and 11 MB estimated District GPU memory. The District frontage atlas is 512 × 512 pixels and the rear and roof atlases are 512 × 256. Final baked output counts and assembled appearance remain to be checked.

A separately owned Cambridge Arcade roof section provides the pitched translucent glazing, ribs, iron ties and lanterns visible over this building's frontage. The adjoining opposite facade is not part of this asset. Its span and structural dimensions are estimated, and the assembled neighbour seam still needs review.

The old Hugo placeholder volumes are removed at publication, while their registered outlines remain as station-area exclusion evidence. A small separately owned polygon extends the existing Christ Church approach floor into the court, uses its retained 0.7845 m grade and subtracts building and tower footprints. A separate estimated 1.8 m frontage transition joins the level floor to the retained rough Chapel pavement, which varies from approximately 0.52 to 0.60 m there. Its outer vertices match the neighbouring exported ground to within 1 micrometre; planar interpolation is checked within 10 mm. The transition is a modelled support, not a surveyed ramp. A bounded paved support spans the BHS section of Cambridge Arcade; a 2 m transition joins the retained station surface to the approach grade. Its limits are estimated; the arcade continuation beyond this section remains unpaved in the model. Neither addition changes the rough terrain controls. The building and ground validators check exact court corner agreement, an unobstructed sky over two court probes, ground ownership and float32 same-facing coplanarity. A native exported check found no positive-area coplanar conflict with the revised approach, station furnishing or Town Hall; this is not a general intersection or visual test.

The building, its canopy and the bounded ground support are locally published. Detail, Walk and District preserve the major massing; District uses baked surfaces and consequently softens the smaller joinery. The construction, carved relief, signage and hidden interfaces remain approximations from the available imagery. Independent final visual acceptance is pending.
