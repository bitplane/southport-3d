# Former BHS, 17–23 Chapel Street

Provisional reconstructed exterior of the connected Chapel Street corner, Cambridge Arcade wing and Corporation Street gabled range. It represents retained fabric visible in published photographs, with temporary conversion works omitted; the proposed completed conversion is not modelled as fact. The Qlocal photographs published in 2025 show the arched and columned Corporation return and the half-timbered service gables. The February 2026 Southport Lead photograph shows scaffolding and a partly stripped canopy soffit; temporary scaffolding is omitted. Images and screenshots remain private. Source links and dated coverage are in `research/coverage.json` and `research/references.json`.

The existing OSM-derived block corners provide registration rather than a survey. The local rectangular construction coordinates span 28.4 × 46.8 m and are mapped bilinearly to the skewed four-corner plan. The gross rectangle is approximately 1,329 m²; Sefton's 2025 assessment gives a 1,340 m² site, a plausibility check only, not a measurement of the building. The wing, frontage and five transverse roofs were compared with four Google 3D bearings and closer imagery. Approximate wing width 14 m, gabled range 8.5 m and their intervening 5.9 m court are hypotheses inferred from obliques, not surveyed measurements. The Town Hall closes the rear boundary; the court is not asserted to be a through passage. Ridge/eave estimates have at least 1–2 m uncertainty. Its concealed junctions, detailed roof materials, individual window measurements, latest post-conversion condition and all hidden interior construction remain unresolved.

The revised master has projecting curved masonry and glazed bays, deep upper galleries, large arched openings, individual roof cutouts and framed dormers, five gabled service bays, and a modelled Chapel canopy. The 19 January 2025 Southport Lead photograph also resolves five framed bays on the near arcade-side elevation, with oval upper grilles, projecting piers, panels and a BHS fascia; the oblique view leaves their metric spacing and damaged infill uncertain. A plain provisional rear roof closure fills the visible gap between the court-side wall and flat roof; its concealed junction with the tower is estimated. Historic features from the 2011 street panorama include the Corporation service door, shutter and timber gables; the unobscured Chapel photographs guide the retained stone frontage. Their capture dates are not confirmed by article publication dates. Contemporary conversion completion is not assumed. The exact carving, signage typography, entrance positions, concealed roof junctions and gallery depths are estimates; the revision schedule records photographic landmarks and uncertainty.

Walk retains the major bays, gallery columns, dormers, gable coves and opening divisions; small leadwork and louvre blades are omitted. District has independently authored curved bays, gallery and arch voids, roof cutouts, court and major silhouette. The bounded limits are 140,000 Detail, 120,000 Walk and 1,200 District triangles, with at most 3 MB District download and 11 MB estimated District GPU memory. The Walk limit allows the visible arcade-side frames and grille divisions. The District frontage atlas is 512 × 512 pixels and the rear and roof atlases are 512 × 256. The exported Detail / Walk / District packages contain 128,351 / 113,379 / 808 triangles, 19,779,480 / 17,583,016 / 427,880 download bytes and an estimated 24,186,537 / 21,994,161 / 8,495,803 GPU bytes.

A separately owned Cambridge Arcade roof section provides the pitched translucent glazing, ribs, iron ties and lanterns visible over this building's frontage. The adjoining opposite facade is not part of this asset. Its span and structural dimensions are estimated, and the assembled neighbour seam still needs review.

The old Hugo placeholder volumes are removed at publication, while their registered outlines remain as station-area exclusion evidence. A small separately owned polygon extends the existing Christ Church approach floor into the court, uses its retained 0.7845 m grade and subtracts building and tower footprints. A separate estimated 1.8 m frontage transition joins the level floor to the retained rough Chapel pavement, which varies from approximately 0.52 to 0.60 m there. The transition is a modelled support, not a surveyed ramp. A bounded paved support spans the BHS section of Cambridge Arcade. Along its station-owned south section, a 2 m wide transition connects the arcade wall and entrance to the rough station floor, which was 0.27–0.41 m lower than the building base. The transition shares its south edge with the Chapel threshold and its north edge with the approach-owned support, which rises to the retained level floor over 2 m. The approach-owned narrow strip at the opposite side of the arcade follows the station floor at their shared boundary. All dimensions and grades are estimates; the arcade continuation beyond this section remains unpaved in the model. Neither addition changes the rough terrain controls. The building and ground validators check court clearance, ground ownership and float32 same-facing coplanarity; these are not general intersection or visual tests.

The building, its canopy and the bounded ground support are locally published. Detail, Walk and District preserve the major massing; District uses baked surfaces and consequently softens the smaller joinery. The construction, carved relief, signage and hidden interfaces remain approximations from the available imagery. Independent final visual acceptance is pending.
