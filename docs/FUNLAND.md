# Silcocks Funland

The Funland asset replaces the Nevill block-plan terminal volume on the Promenade
forecourt, between Marine Way and Southport Pier. The carousel is a separate,
explicitly provisional pavilion volume in the same asset; its ride, horses and
ornament are deferred.

Fresh displayed Google 3D was orbited in four directions and compared with an
overhead plan. August 2024 Google Street View covers front, north and rear;
May 2011 close pier-side imagery and a partly loaded May 2017 panorama cover
persistent south-side architecture. A displayed official exterior photo uploaded
in July 2024 and davidsong's 2019 Mapillary frontage corroborate the scalloped
front and offset white carousel. Source links, dates, access failures and missing
coverage are recorded under the asset's `research/` directory. Reference captures
remain private, outside source control.

The registered envelope is approximately 29.1 × 59 m. Eight cream scalloped arches,
scalloped parapet, red shopfronts, brick piers, cream cladding and readable signs
establish the frontage. Separate cross-front, low linking, small raised pier-side
and rear hall roof volumes preserve the researched silhouette. The rear service
strip remains screened; no hidden equipment or interiors are invented. Metric
heights and shallow roof falls remain estimates, with approximately 0.8–1.5 m
uncertainty. The floor is level at 3.33 m local datum, tied to the existing rough
editable pavement; no terrain raster reconstruction or deferred pier/restaurant
terrace connection is included.

Detail retains shallow arch profiles, cladding seams and small lamp beads. Walk
omits the thin seams, lamp beads and vent slats. District is an independent fixed
roof/parapet envelope with a 512² architecture atlas, plus a 256² provisional
carousel atlas. Its triangle ceilings are 600/200; total download and decoded GPU
ceilings are 1.8 MB and 6 MB. Added lettering and ornament affect baked appearance,
not the coarse topology or atlas dimensions. Dark opaque glazing represents the
unresearched interior boundary.

Build and review results are recorded in `research/review.json`; generated mesh,
projection and exported surface reports stay in `build/`.

On 26 October 2026 the actual local viewer loaded Detail, Walk and District at the
same front viewpoint, then an assembled rear/roof view with the pier, Marine Way,
forecourt and carousel. District retains readable main lettering and roof/parapet
silhouette, with expected loss of thin arch depth and small fascia text. Carousel
Detail/Walk share identical geometry and therefore the viewer reports their
collapsed tile as Detail at both settings. No asset-loading failures occurred.
The initial close oblique viewer review caught bright slivers behind frame rims;
inner frame reveals now close the depth to the recessed panes. The provisional
carousel enclosure has a below-grade footing while its roof stays level.

The first complete package measured 29,943 / 29,288 / 646 triangles for
Detail / Walk / District, before the small frame-depth correction. All source
meshes and exported levels passed the coplanar checks. A separate cropped audit
of the actual transformed exports found no cross-asset coplanar overlap with
pier entrance/deck/fittings, Nevill/Marine Drive/south-lake ground or adjacent
placeholder candidates. This is scoped surface evidence, not a claim that the
whole surrounding terrain has been resolved. Detailed lower restaurant terrace
and promenade connections remain explicitly deferred.

Final corrected package: **30,055 Detail / 29,400 Walk / 646 District triangles**;
GLB downloads total **2,783,508 / 2,705,592 / 179,096 bytes** respectively.
District estimated GPU allocation is 5,331,053 bytes, including both three-map
atlases. All 26 source meshes classify exactly once. The repeated frame-depth
fix, carousel footing, final render stage and exported/nearby surface checks pass.
Walking on the existing forecourt gave an eye height of 5.028 m (1.7 m above its
rough support), consistent with the chosen building floor. This does not validate
or change the deferred lower waterfront route.
