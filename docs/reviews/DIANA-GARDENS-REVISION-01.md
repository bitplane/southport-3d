## Priority 1: measured layout and levels

Confirm the two pavilion footprints, roof proportions, fountain centre and
diameters, patterned axis width, perimeter boundaries, planting beds, entrances
and rear service/parking access. Existing coordinates and OSM outlines are seeds,
not measurements. Save an annotated metric schedule with source, method and
uncertainty. Use multiple views to distinguish circular bands, actual risers and
perspective effects; the reserved 10 m radius is not an observed fountain size.

Keep simple editable grade controls. A basin needs a horizontal water surface,
with deliberate surrounding slopes, steps and accessible connections. Do not
drape it over the rough road height grid or flatten the whole neighbourhood.
Verify adjoining Town Hall approach and Eastbank junction heights. Provide a
plan, two opposing obliques and a street-height axial view matched to evidence
before detailed repetition or expensive baking. Send these to root for review.

## Priority 2: complete the defining architecture

Replace both pavilion placeholders (ways 371196091 and 1370054624) with separately
researched exteriors. Determine each footprint, roof facets/pitch/ridge or apex,
eaves, slender posts, opening rhythm, glazing versus open bays, doors, plinth and
roof trim. Do not assume the two buildings are identical. Inspect all sides and
record unseen parts rather than inventing ornament or illegible text. Show each
pavilion from at least two sides and above at a useful comparison scale.

Build fountain basin, coping, concentric paving and evidenced changes in level.
Match axial paving borders, cell scale and alignment; arbitrary checker cells
must not dictate the composition. Add observed balustrade runs, piers, caps,
entrance openings and any evidenced urns. Prototype one characteristic column,
globe lamp and balustrade panel before repeating. Benches should use the shared
model, with evidenced placement and orientation. Include bounded planting beds,
tree placement, kerbs and immediate service-route/pavement connections. Avoid
generic furniture scattered to make the site look busy.

dedicated architectural assets where appropriate, remove replaced volumes once,
and preserve unrelated corridor geometry. Coordinate shared-file edits with root.

Use explicit surface ownership, subtract patterned inserts, avoid floating

stonework. Keep furniture separate from walkable surfaces. Add scoped checks
for actual observed failure risks. Design Detail/Walk plus fixed-budget District
envelopes with documented triangle/texture budgets; added fine detail must not
increase coarse cost. Preserve pavilion silhouette and principal openings.

Use scoped commits, never absorb another worker's staged files.

Send root checkpoints with exact image paths and remaining discrepancies. Do not
