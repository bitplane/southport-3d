# 11–21 London Street

The three former grey context boxes now represent William Hill (11–13), KBA/Yorkshire Building Society (15–17) and David H Myers (19–21). The stable asset ID retains `east-side-placeholders` for existing viewer links. Occupier sources establish addresses; inconsistent map labels do not control the building names.

The ground-plan divisions remain registered to the neighbouring Tudor frontage and Anchor Street. Google 3D views from south, west and north establish the roof forms: William Hill has a sloping front roof and a narrower pitched rear range; KBA has one transverse hipped main roof with projecting front gables and lower rear extensions; Myers has a front hip, rear gable, front dormer, two Anchor Street dormers and a broad party-side roof interruption.

August 2024 Street View establishes KBA's canted bays, timber gables, recessed entrance and 1989 plaque, and Myers' four upper sashes, brick parapet, spectacles fascia, rendered side and rear garage. William Hill uses 2019 contributor imagery checked against adjoining 2024 views. Shop interiors, temporary window advertising and unreadable notices are omitted.

Each building is a separate streaming part. Detail retains trim and lettering; Walk omits small detail and preserves major openings and roof geometry; District bakes sign panels while retaining the inexpensive silhouette and recessed glazing. No downloaded reference pixels are distributed. Source textures are authored procedural masonry and roofing.

The comparison cameras are fitted perspective views, not recovered survey positions. The auction's gross shop frontage is not assumed to equal the whole William Hill building width. Ground dimensions are approximate, roof heights are estimated, and hidden service elevations remain plain where evidence is missing. See `research/block-review/coverage.json`, `measurements.json` and `elevation-calibration.py` beside the asset for inputs and limitations. Mapillary imagery failed to load during this research; that is not a claim that it has no coverage.
