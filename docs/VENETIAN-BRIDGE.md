# Venetian Bridge and islands

The Venetian Bridge crosses the south Marine Lake between Kings Gardens and
Princes Park. This reconstruction replaces the former solid parapet envelopes
with open cream balustrades, green island guardrails, panelled piers, green columns, transverse timber deck,
a central arch and flatter supported end spans. Two separately outlined islands
have low crowns, sloping rock-coloured banks and estimated planting masses. The
water is a separate non-walkable surface, cut around the same island outlines.

[Stephen Craven's June 2023 top view](https://www.geograph.org.uk/photo/7531530)
and [side view](https://www.geograph.org.uk/photo/7531531), a
[Sefton close side view](https://www.sefton.gov.uk/media/3068/bridge_close_up.jpg)
and [wide side view](https://www.sefton.gov.uk/media/3066/venetian_bridge.jpg),
[another side view](https://tmpdmsmedia.newmindmedia.com/wsimgs/Kings-Gardens-52_1079120730.jpg),
and loaded overhead and opposing Google Maps 3D views informed the work. The
transverse boards, cream turned balusters, square inset-panel piers and green
columns are visible in the 2023 view. The side-mounted globe and socket represent its clearly visible foreground lamp.
A second oblique photograph published in 2025 (capture date unknown) shows several
centred globes and some bare posts. The model retains a bounded representative
selection of those heads; combining observations does not establish their exact
simultaneous or current condition. In 2025 Sefton reported bridge-light repairs. The exact number and spacing of
columns, panel widths, rocky banks and individual planting positions are estimates.
Private images are not distributed with the model.

The registered straight bridge axis is approximately 162.6 m long and its 5.2 m
overall width is an aerial estimate. The deck, transverse-board UVs, fascia,
rail and plinths now share this frame and the same longitudinal height profile.
The previous deck used a bent centreline offset by up to 0.63 m from the rails.
Pier caps align with the outer deck edge, plinth bases follow the grade and the
base rails meet the deck; terminal piers bound the approach rails. Green metal
rail sections across the islands follow the oblique view, with transition
stations and head locations estimated. The central deck and rail rise about 1.1 m above the flat
spans; the arch soffit is a separate curve. The fixed lake datum is unchanged.
Pixel ratios in the side image suggested about 1.8–2.3 rail heights between
flat deck and water; the authored model uses about 1.9–2.0. The station table,
image coverage, assumptions and fixed budgets are in
`research/venetian-evidence.json` and `research/layout.json`.

The Kings Gardens approach blends into the rough surrounding ground over a
bounded grade, which also limits the shore-wall top at the bridge crossing. At the Princes Park end, a separate lower path crosses under the
bridge; one green side gate and an estimated stair link it to the deck. The gate
is visible in the 2023 photo and nearby steps in the loaded 3D view, but their
precise position and dimensions are not surveyed. Under-deck clearance is an
estimated 1.79 m, not a verified measured clearance or accessibility claim.
Additional island gate locations and concealed structure remain unverified.

The bridge deck, lower path, island ground and lake water have separate ownership.
Detail retains turned balusters; Walk uses reduced turned profiles; District uses
fewer open posts rather than a solid wall. Coarse topology does not grow with
fine baluster detail. The single green-gate/stair connection is retained across
levels. The main build and its complete coplanar audit passed. Native checks include
3,578 deck, rail and plinth contacts plus an unobstructed Kings approach. Each
published level passed 1,964 bridge contact probes and 3,363 water-coverage
probes. The scoped exported overlap scan found no cross-asset groups; the seven
deck fan-seam residues were at most 0.12 mm wide. In the current viewer, walking
advanced above and below the bridge at District and switching the lower view to
Walk and Detail preserved its support height. The earlier reconstruction also
recorded full bridge crossings at all three levels. A broad legacy neighbour
seam checker stopped at a separate 0.119 m paving/asphalt boundary at site
(-200, 65); that unrelated finding is not certified by this work.

The current local increment is pending independent visual acceptance; viewer
snags `074c5aaaff9d4a09baace83cce103975` and
`0c6d2d7b226345dd9bdf5d680d2142d0` remain in progress. Terrain, rail,
structure, gates and lighting are a researched reconstruction, not a survey.
