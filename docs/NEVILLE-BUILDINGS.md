# Neville Buildings / Empire Restaurant, 11–13 Nevill Street

Asset: `southport.nevill-street.nevill-street-11-13`.

The current restaurant occupies the ornate red-brick range immediately north of
3–9 Nevill Street. Sefton's Townscape Heritage list identifies it as Neville
Buildings; the Food Standards Agency verifies Empire's 11–13 address. The
neighbour's terminal pediment and pale doorway remain part of 3–9, not Empire.

## Evidence and placement

The evidence record links July 2026 Google Street View, an August 2019 VR360TV
panorama, four actual Google 3D orbit directions, a flat overhead calibration and
the Lancashire Archives drawing reproduced by Southport BID. Captures are kept
outside Git. The historical drawing explains the facade rhythm but is not the
current state: the corner spire is absent, and the central
gable's former tall window has been largely infilled. A small low sash remains.

Four coordinate-labelled map pixels constrain the approximately 9.67 m frontage
and 11.10 m depth. The adopted origin shares 3–9's exact terminal party boundary;
the approximately 2 m disagreement between elevated Google roof registration
and the existing block datum is recorded rather than concealed by moving one
building. Heights, carved depths and rear openings are estimates. The separate
`west-nevill-empire` placeholder is replaced, and both dependent ground assets
are rebuilt to preserve the rounded corner and narrow passage beside Salaars.

The current front is well covered. The side and rear are available only in
coarse photogrammetry: paired upper openings are approximate and hidden doors,
private yard fittings, drainage and plant are deferred. A nearby Mapillary image
faces the opposite building. A Cable Street panorama search resolved to the
Ship & Anchor's courtyard and was excluded. No image of that different building
was treated as Empire evidence.

## Model and LoDs

Detail and Walk preserve the seven lower and five upper main sash axes, curved
corner windows, recessed glazing, broken and segmental pediments, central
balcony, continuous iron guard, curved balcony supports, upper draped rails,
stepped gable and two dormers. The hip roof and its rooflights follow the 3D
close views. Four capped stacks, the small hip plateau, extended pitched gable
hood and five rear roof windows are evidenced by a separate close 48 m orbit;
wide block imagery alone did not resolve them. Rear masonry is pale and weathered.
Text and the visible Chinese fascia characters use authored fonts; small
unreadable advertising print is omitted. Carvings and the buffet logotype are
approximations, not scans or reproductions of photographic pixels. Brick, roof
and granite maps are procedurally authored.

District is a separate surface envelope. Its 512-square front atlas and
256-square rear/roof atlases bake colour, normal and roughness. Budgets are 400
front and roof triangles, 300 rear triangles, 2 MB download and 7 MB estimated
GPU storage; adding ornament does not enlarge the receiver mesh or atlases.
The curved corner, tiled hip and three front roof features survive. Railings,
window recesses and carved relief become baked appearance. Regional distant
proxies are generated by the existing 3D Tiles pipeline.

## Surface ownership and review

The wall uses actual window apertures. Closely grouped stone surrounds are
clipped at shared divider boundaries; their hidden seam caps are omitted.
Curved corner reveals, glazing and stone bands share radial endpoints instead
of overlapping independently extruded chords. Mitred gable caps, separated
sash rails and split cornices avoid coincident faces. No large Boolean repair
is required.

The validator checks finite/nondegenerate geometry, closed outward hip slabs,
seven unobstructed lower-window probes, packed textures and coplanar overlap
across architectural meshes and non-iron ornament. An additional native scan of
the exported complete model checks the source detector's limits. Three tiny
self-overlap groups remain at ornamental iron-loop welds, approximately
0.0004 square metres of summed triangle-pair area; these are documented joins,
not an exemption for the facade. Exported District and neighbour seams are
reviewed separately. `research/review.json` records the final reference and
runtime checks and their limitations.

## Published review

The current Detail and Walk exports each contain 49,035 triangles and total
7,399,048 bytes, with 12,765,442 bytes estimated GPU storage. District contains
198 triangles, totals 500,008 bytes, and uses an estimated 6,316,755 GPU bytes.
The source validator passes. The complete exported native overlap scan across
this asset, 3–9 Nevill, the remaining placeholders and both adjoining ground
assets finds no cross-asset overlaps involving Empire. Only the documented
iron-loop weld group and four float32 District gable-edge slivers remain.

Actual exported Detail, Walk and District were inspected from front and rear
views after the close-orbit roof correction. Private review captures are in
`../output/nevill-empire/final-*.png`; the isolated comparison is served at
`/reviews/southport.nevill-street.nevill-street-11-13/index.html`.
