# St George’s block layout

The working block runs from NatWest and the Posting House along St George’s Place to St George’s Church, bounded behind by Anchor Street and at the north end by Hill Street / Castle Street. Its ground and provisional rear buildings belong to `southport.london-square.public-realm`; church garden surfaces retain their existing separate ownership.

The retained [block plan](../regions/southport/london-square/public-realm/research/st-georges-block.json) contains attributed OpenStreetMap geometry in the shared metric frame. It is compared with Google satellite imagery and the privately supplied Posting House aerial. These are wall-footprint controls, not a measured survey; roof overhangs and canopies need independent interpretation.

| Building | Plan control and remaining simplification |
| --- | --- |
| Posting House | OSM 371741772 historic frontage; 653815606 tapered redevelopment. The rear wall has its own skew. Modern front inset and courtyard subdivisions remain photographic estimates. |
| St George’s Mansions | OSM 371742617 street edge and west rear wing; side direction is independent of facade direction. Other wing subdivision dimensions remain approximate. |
| Sainsbury’s | OSM 371203338 outer four corners, rather than a perpendicular rectangle. Small west-wall step remains simplified. |
| St George’s Church | OSM 51826176 orientation retained across nave, tower and hall. Hall depth and heights remain estimates. |
| Rear buildings | OSM 371742618, 371742619, 371742620 and 51826175 are plain provisional volumes with estimated heights. Replace them on these footprints when architectural evidence is researched. |

NatWest and the adjoining 15 London Street building share a coarse OSM outline; the separate architectural evidence determines their internal division. The concurrent 15 London Street task owns that division and alley, so this pass does not override it.

Sainsbury’s parking follows OSM 42018443. Parking rows, stall dimensions, pedestrian connections, low boundary walls and furniture placements are estimates from aerial imagery. The three surrounding street centrelines retain their mapped bends. Road widths are estimates. Open mouths connect the car park, Anchor Street, Hill Street and Castle Street; ownership boundaries are not closed with kerbs.

Ground parts retain the same outlines at Detail, Walk and District, with smaller authored textures at distance. Temporary building volumes remain simple at every level. Reusable furniture uses its existing generator reductions. Architecture keeps its existing per-feature LoD decisions after registration.

Future extensions start with this kind of block plan and placeholder review before detailed building work; see [the modelling workflow](MODELLING-WORKFLOW.md).
