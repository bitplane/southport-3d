# Rights and provenance

A project-wide code/asset licence has not yet been selected. Public visibility alone does not grant permission to reuse all repository content. A licence decision is needed before advertising this as an openly licensed asset library or accepting contributions on that basis.

Reference photographs and captured web pages are not distributed here. The memorial's research/reference_catalog.json records source pages and available credits/licence notes. Those entries describe the references, not a blanket licence for this project. Links may move or require access.

The construction scripts and numeric relief surfaces describe a researched reconstruction. Relief surfaces include authored sculpting, traced forms and bounded shading-derived detail from references; they are not photographs, but excluding photographs does not settle rights in the depicted sculptures or derived work. Generated textures are procedural. Review provenance and applicable rights before publishing model downloads under a chosen licence.

Site layout uses OpenStreetMap-derived measurements: © OpenStreetMap contributors. OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL); see https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Preserve attribution and assess obligations when redistributing derived geographic datasets.

Other source institutions and photographers retain their respective rights. Missing or uncertain attribution should be corrected in the source catalogue, without importing the reference image.
