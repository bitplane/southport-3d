# Evidence and provisional dimensions

Google satellite identifies the marked isolated parcel as Pizza Hut, between the retail car park and Marine Parade. Existing OSM outline way 52902208 is a hypothesis; its four vertices span 17.4 × 20.8 m, with nominal uncertainty ±1–2 m. The apparent roof overhang adds about 0.5 m. The neighbouring access path and parking remain under the public-realm owner. The doorway gives a rough 2.2 m height control: street-view proportions suggest wall/eaves 3.2–3.5 m and top of raised roof 5.7 m (each ±0.7 m). This is not a measured survey.

The March 2019 panorama camera is north-east of the building, facing south-west; the entrance is accordingly on the parking-facing north-east edge. The August 2018 service panorama is south-west of the building. March 2019 parking-side Street View shows the centred glazed entrance with a curved shallow projecting canopy, timber right-hand surround and large windows to the right; the left end is buff block. August 2018 rear view shows buff block and two service doors, projecting dark fascia and the raised sign band. August 2024 road view shows glazing on the adjoining side. The aerial shows the building standing independently; GPU-enabled Google 3D close opposing orbits show a recessed plant deck enclosed by the raised sign band, with plant volumes and an entrance canopy on the parking side. Initial default browser reported 3D unavailable. Equipment detail, detailed fourth elevation and interior remain uncertain. Photographs are not textures. The wordmark is approximated with text; exact lettering and plant equipment remain provisional.
