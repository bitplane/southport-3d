# 188 Lord Street: preliminary evidence, 27 October 2026

Scope is the two matching towers between Court Road and Hoghton Grove and the separate three-storey block to their rear. Regent Court / Farmfoods at the Court Road corner is not part of this asset. Google Maps satellite and GPU 3D orbit (2026 imagery, accessed 2026-10-27), Street View August 2024 and August 2018, Maro's development page and the sale photographs below were inspected. The GPU child browser was needed to enable Globe and 3D.

- [Maro development](https://marodev.co.uk/development/188-lord-street-southport/) describes the courtyard buildings and separate rear block. Its nine 360×280 images include interiors; the images inspected were not evidence for exterior form.
- [Street View, Court Road, August 2024](https://www.google.com/maps/@53.6513843,-2.999475,10a,90y,120h,105t/data=!3m7!1e1!3m5!1syyx_mkeL681mEez5P5DpWg!2e0!7i16384!8i8192) shows red brick, buff stone, white window frames, metal-framed clear glass balustrades, stone entrance with glass blocks, and projected balconies. A tree hides part of the frontage.
- [Front sale photograph](https://www.rightmove.co.uk/properties/161935595) shows the whole end, entrance, front tower, and rear tower beyond. There are six principal storeys including ground and an upper recessed penthouse, not seven equal-height red storeys. The main end has three long central balcony bands and a narrow right balcony column on each upper floor, a strip of stone and individual windows separating them. The bands are on five floors above ground; right balconies begin at ground. The broad eaves step around the inset penthouse. Photograph perspective and tree obscure some left bays.
- [Rear-block auction photograph](https://propertyauctions.io/listings/19bed3e1fdf2b4fa38c4b878e5536bf6) shows the landscaped garden elevation: three storeys, central brick panel with two paired French windows at each of two lower levels, a recessed pale third storey with continuous balcony, flanking narrow side balconies, white frames and an entrance right of centre. The 3D orbit shows a dark hipped roof, visible as a single long block in line with and behind the towers.
- [2004 application site plan](https://modgov.sefton.gov.uk/moderngov/Data/Planning%20Committee/20040602/Agenda/Appendix%2009.pdf) records 61 units in two seven-storey blocks; this is proposal evidence, not verification of exact as-built form.

Existing OSM-based local site footprints are provisional registration, not a survey: front tower `way-372055262` approximately x=442.7–470.5, y=-27.3–-9.8; middle tower `way-372055263` x=441.9–469.5, y=-56.3–-40.2; third block `way-1041189125` approximately x=450.3–462.5, y=-105.3–-74.1. Site metres use the corridor origin and 43.5293° rotation. Distance between first two footprints is roughly 13 m; rear gap roughly 18 m. These are seed polygon ranges, not finished measurements. The front and middle roofs are approximately 28×17 m and show perimeter ledges plus central raised structures. The model estimates a 1.5 m frontal top-storey setback, 0.8 m roof overhang and 8×4.8 m central roof housing; these are image-derived estimates, not surveyed measurements. Their exact floor heights, setbacks, side and courtyard elevations need measurement and matching. Rear block has 12×31 m footprint; the garden faces the Manchester Road side. The service face is visible in an opposing aerial orbit but individual glazing divisions are too soft to count reliably. All heights are approximate until calibrated.
