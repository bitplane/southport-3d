## Representation

Wing tracery has its own generator, distinct from tower roundels. Branching
mouldings are unioned in their plane before building continuous skins and exposed
edges. Recessed glazing is behind the stonework. Detail has connected diamond
leading; Walk retains the same upper tracery with fewer curve samples and omits
fine leading. District retains its independent fixed-budget envelope and rebakes
the revised appearance. The two matching doors receive full-height centre stiles,
continuous arched timber heads, separate lower panels and properly meeting rails.
The two wing masonry faces use continuous skins and explicit reveals; internal
clipping-fragment caps that shimmered below the windows are removed.
