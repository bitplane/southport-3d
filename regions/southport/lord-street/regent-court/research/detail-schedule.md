# Feature schedule

Before multiplication: compare one full-height bay and the shop entrance with
August 2024 Court Road imagery. Four visible faces: paired brick residential
wings around recessed central slot, white-framed windows, projecting balconies
with light rails. End wall windows continue the storey rhythm. Two-level concrete
podium has shop glazing and entrance, sign and gated open parking above. Flat
projecting pale roof has recessed roof plane, offset service block and openings.
Adjacent low service wing and access gap must be checked in assembled scene.

Detail retains frames, reveals, balcony slabs and railings, ground entrance and
shop sign, concrete parking beams, piers and gates, roof perimeter and access
block. Walk retains building silhouette, balcony slab and simplified rails,
major glazing/entrance and signage; fine bars and handles can be omitted.
District is an independently authored fixed topology tower/podium/roof envelope,
retaining central indentation and major roof outline. Balcony and window colour
is projected onto bounded surfaces. Declared caps: 700 triangles tower, 160 roof,
512² atlas per part, combined 2.5 MB download and 12 MB estimated GPU. Verify
atlas floor and adjust only from measured constraints before baking.

Source of repeated pitch is the visually counted ten residential rows, estimate
2.8 m; retail and parking podium total 6.5 m. Exact aperture sizes, rooftop
fittings and individual balcony rail variants are provisional until measured
against views. Sign must reproduce only legible 'farmfoods', no invented copy.
